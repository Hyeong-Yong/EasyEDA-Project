제작 후 필수 검증

  • Nominal TI macro-model, ideal DAC/reference and supply rails, 27C only.
  • AC uses documented optran 0 0 0 20n 100u 0 initialization; first direct-OP/DC convergence failures are retained, not counted as passing DC sweeps.
  • Four slow full-range transient plateaus replace failed direct DC sweeps; resistor corners are separate ideal linear algebra.
  • No validated TPS65131 switching/startup simulation, no relay release/flyback or hot-plug/contact-bounce qualification, no Monte Carlo silicon/thermal hardware proof.
  • Power-good truth-table/window arithmetic is not a transistor-level power-sequence simulation.
  • No hardware has been built or measured. No unconditional production/safety/accuracy certification.
  • J1 is a retained generic 2x5 female header; mating geometry and exact stock part need procurement confirmation.
  • Susumu precision resistors and explicitly manual YAGEO parts must be separately sourced; assembly/BOM inventory and final quotes are not guaranteed.
  • PGOOD is a rail window, not an accuracy flag. Lower rail limits do not guarantee full +/-10V op-amp headroom. AVDD upper threshold worst-case can reach 5.545V; normal LDO tolerance envelope is below 5.5V, but this window is not an overvoltage clamp.
  • TPS65131 peak switch-current limit vs L1/L2 saturation, MLCC effective capacitance, input inrush, startup/shutdown ripple and LDO thermal headroom need prototype qualification.
  • STM32 DAC cable capacitance above its <=50pF buffer specification needs a source-side buffer; receiver buffer does not remove cable capacitance. Outputs are high impedance-load drivers, not 50-ohm termination drivers.
  • A relay is not instantaneous. Test muted startup, power failure, contact bounce and input-cable disconnection before attaching external precision equipment.
  • Native PNG/SVG export times out in connected EasyEDA 3.2.149. Cached project thumbnails are not asserted as current final schematic visual proof. Current source geometry, saved/reopened ERC and portable export topology were independently verified.
